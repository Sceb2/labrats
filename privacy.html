LabRats: your information

LabRats helps you practise Chemistry and Applied Science. Your school uses your information to support your learning, as part of its educational role.

What we keep. Your school gives you a username and temporary password. You choose your own password when you first sign in. You do not need to give LabRats your real name, email address, date of birth or phone number. Your school may keep a separate record linking your username to you, so your results are still personal information. LabRats uses a made-up email identifier internally; it does not receive email.

When signed in, we keep your classes and subjects, account and sign-in dates, questions and flashcards attempted, marks, hints/reveals, retries and flashcard self-assessments. We do not save your selected answers or typed responses in your account history. A–E ratings help you revise; they are not exam grades.

Who can see it. Teachers see results for their assigned classes and subjects. School administrators manage accounts. Supabase provides the account/database service and GitHub Pages hosts the website. Service security logs may include your IP address and device/browser information. External videos or links have their own privacy information.

How long. Student accounts and their practice history are scheduled for deletion two years after first successful login, normally completed within seven days. Accounts never used are scheduled after 90 days from creation. The school can delete accounts earlier or approve a documented, time-limited extension.

Guest practice. You can practise without saving results to an account. Active Learning progress stays in that browser; guest flashcard results last only for the current page. Sign out on shared devices. Unsaved offline account work can be lost if you close the tab or clear its storage.

Need help? Forgotten your username or password? Speak to Mr Wormstone in person. Never email your password. For questions about your information, or to ask to see, correct or delete it, contact sceb2@crispins.co.uk. The school will explain your rights and how to contact its Data Protection Officer. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
