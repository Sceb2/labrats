## Explicit gaps

- Wider alkane properties/combustion and environmental organohalogen chemistry; full mechanistic equation variety for radical substitution.
- Full alkene polymerisation and sustainability coverage.
- Detailed haloalkane hydrolysis-rate practical, bond-enthalpy comparisons and environmental questions.
- Preparation/purification apparatus, separating funnels, drying, redistillation, recrystallisation, reduced-pressure filtration and melting-point procedures.
- Full benzene evidence/delocalisation, relative reactivity, phenol acidity, directing effects and multi-substituent route planning.
- Carbonyl analytical tests (2,4-DNP and Tollens), detailed identification and spectroscopic content.
- Full amine basicity, amino acid reactions, chirality, condensation polymers and hydrolysis.
- Broad acid–base reactions, acid-anhydride methods, secondary amide formation and all acyl-chloride alternatives.
- Open route design, full mechanism drawing for every prescribed family, and arbitrary unfamiliar contexts.
