# Teacher testing and chemistry review

## Five-minute functional check
1. Select only Alcohols. Confirm questions needing both Alcohols and another topic do not appear.
2. Select all topics and all six types. Confirm 36 eligible questions.
3. Start twice and check question/option order changes while marking remains correct.
4. Complete a session, refresh, and confirm selections persist.
5. Reset device data and confirm selections/progress clear.
6. Start a challenge, background the tab briefly, return, and confirm time uses the real end timestamp.
7. Use Tab, Space and Enter only; check visible focus and usable selection.
8. Test around 360 px width and 200% browser zoom.

## Chemistry judgements requiring a teacher
- Confirm each `specRefs` entry against the centre's working copy of OCR H432 v3.1.
- Check distractors are chemically wrong in the exact context, not merely unusual wording.
- Review distinctions between aqueous/ethanolic, distillation/reflux, dilute/concentrated and catalyst/reagent.
- Confirm options do not combine components from different valid methods.
- Confirm every arrow starts at a bond, lone pair or negative charge and ends at the intended atom/bond.
- Approve condensed structural formulae and convert them to reviewed SVGs before broad rollout.
- Change `status` to `teacher-approved` only after review.

## Ambiguity review
- RC01/RC02 deliberately name both reagent and heating method to prevent hidden marking requirements.
- RC06 follows OCR wording (CN⁻ and ethanol) and should be checked against how your department teaches KCN/NaCN.
- MS04 is intentionally narrow but should be replaced in Stage 2 with a fully drawn route.
- Mechanism-completion items use explicit source→destination wording and reject reversed arrows.
- Nomenclature distractors avoid accepted synonyms; NM04 includes “bromopropane” as insufficiently located, not as a competing systematic name.
