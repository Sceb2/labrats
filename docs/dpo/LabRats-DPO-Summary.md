# LabRats — data processing and security summary
4 November 2026 · Development implementation; hosted activation and school approval pending

**Purpose and responsibility.** LabRats supports Chemistry and Applied Science revision in one school. One student account works across subjects; question and self-assessed flashcard progress remain separate. Adaptive N/A–E guidance prioritises revision; it is not an exam grade or a basis for significant automated decisions. The school/trust must confirm its controller role, lawful basis, privacy information and DPIA outcome.

**Data minimisation.** Students use random adjective–animal usernames and generated, non-deliverable @students.invalid identifiers. No student name, real email, date of birth or phone number is requested. Supabase Auth holds password hashes and authentication records. Application records include account IDs, roles, class/subject memberships, login times, question/card versions, timestamps, marks and reveal/retry flags. Selected answers and free-text responses are excluded from account storage and uploads. Staff email addresses and administrative activity are processed. Retention extensions record an administrator, reason and expiry; reasons should contain no sensitive information. These records are pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data. Any school-held identity mapping needs separate protection.

**Access and security.** No public registration is intended. Administrators invite staff; teachers privately issue batch-created student credentials. First/reset passwords require replacement; the agreed minimum is ten characters. Database row-level security and server checks restrict students to their own history and teachers to assigned classes and subjects; administrators manage the school. Resets revoke sessions. Privileged keys remain server-side; browsers receive public configuration only. HTTPS protects network transport. Marks are client-reported revision evidence, not tamper-proof assessment results.

**Browser and external processing.** Account sessions, metadata caches and offline queues use tab-scoped storage with memory fallback. Responses stay in page memory. Unsynced work can be lost when a tab/storage is lost; sign-out clears account caches and warns about pending work. Guest question history is browser-local; guest flashcard results are memory-only. Optional videos/links contact external providers; user-initiated question-report email drafts may contain answers and identify the sender. Supabase and the website host also process technical/security logs. Database region and all processing/transfer arrangements require confirmation.

**Agreed retention.** Students: two calendar years from first successful login; never-used accounts: 90 days from creation, with weekly deletion within seven further days. Administrators may document an extension or delete earlier; no advance student warning is planned. Deletion removes Auth accounts, memberships and both learning histories. Staff access is revoked on recorded departure, with handover and pack transfer; deletion is due after 23 days for weekly completion within 30. Invitation acceptance is capped at seven days (provider codes may expire sooner); abandoned invitation accounts are deleted within 30 days. Minimal audit/cleanup records, including pseudonymous staff actor references, are retained 12 months, with weekly removal. Existing accounts are excluded from automation pending review.

**Operational boundaries and launch actions.** Retention migration, scheduling and explicit enablement are required; school failure-alert delivery remains unconfigured. Optional GitHub Actions monitoring reads job status and can notify a school-approved endpoint. Local automated tests do not establish legal compliance or verify hosted settings. Before real-student rollout: complete contracts/transfers and supplier backup/log retention review; confirm staff MFA, admin ownership, secure credential disposal, rights/incident procedures and deletion after backup restoration; verify deployed permissions and resolve the deferred guest/account navigation issue. Provider backups/logs, school identity mappings and downloaded exports require separate retention procedures.
