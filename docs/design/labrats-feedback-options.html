What C proposes

  • Put the earned score first. Use restrained visual feedback and specific marking points.
  • Show an earned mark as earned; put the correction beside the part that needs work.
  • Keep full-mark detail collapsed, with optional explanations below.
  • Label retries, revealed answers and method marks explicitly. Keep first-attempt scoring and adaptive rules unchanged.
  • Keep the question one tap away. No changes to celebration settings are included in this preview.
  • Selected answers shown in live feedback already exist during the session; this proposal does not add them to account storage.
