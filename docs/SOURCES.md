## Requirement distinctions

| Classification | Treatment in this release |
|---|---|
| Reactions to know | Reagent/product/synthesis tasks cite reaction outcomes; numeric industrial conditions are not silently required. |
| Prescribed mechanisms | Alkene electrophilic addition (4.1.3(h)); primary haloalkane aqueous hydrolysis (4.2.2(c)); arene nitration/halogenation (6.1.1(e)); carbonyl NaBH₄/HCN addition (6.1.2(c)); cyanide haloalkane chain extension (6.2.4(b)(i)); radical equations (4.1.2(f)). Coverage is partial, rather than a claim of complete drawing practice. |
| Explicitly no mechanism required | Alcohol dehydration 4.2.1(d), and alcohol substitution by halide/acid 4.2.1(e). MI09/MI10 use reaction descriptions and are counted separately from 12 named-mechanism tasks. |
| Supplied unfamiliar application | RC16 supplies the alumina/vapour protocol; 4.2.3(c) permits an extended toolkit from extra information. MI12 interprets a supplied phenol substitution pattern; 6.1.1(g) supports unfamiliar electrophilic substitution interpretation. |
| Other reactions with no prescribed drawing outcome | Acyl chloride, ester and amine transformations are assessed as known reaction/synthesis choices where represented. Do not re-label these as OCR's explicit “mechanism not required” guidance unless the source says so. |
| Structural knowledge / sites | Nomenclature and chemical-site tasks support organic knowledge and mechanism understanding, but do not themselves prove full mechanism-drawing coverage. |
