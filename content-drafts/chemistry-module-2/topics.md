# Module 2: Foundations in chemistry — topic list draft

## 2.2 Electrons, bonding and structure

Each numbered topic is approximately one lesson. The attached OCR scheme of work suggests 3 hours for 2.2.1 and 10 hours for 2.2.2; the sequence below uses those as a starting point. Timings can be adjusted to suit lesson length and class needs. References identify the relevant specification sections, not learning outcomes.

### 2.2.1 Electron structure

1. Electron shells, sub-shells and atomic orbitals — 2.2.1(a–b)
2. Filling orbitals and electron configurations of atoms — 2.2.1(c), 2.2.1(d)(i)
3. Electron configurations of ions — 2.2.1(d)(ii)

### 2.2.2 Bonding and structure

1. Ionic bonding, dot-and-cross diagrams and giant ionic lattices — 2.2.2(a–b)
2. Properties of ionic compounds — 2.2.2(c)
3. Covalent and coordinate bonding, dot-and-cross diagrams and bond strength — 2.2.2(d–f)
4. Molecular shapes and bond angles: bonding pairs — 2.2.2(g–h)
5. Molecular and ion shapes: lone pairs and electron pair repulsion — 2.2.2(g–h)
6. Electronegativity, bond polarity and molecular polarity — 2.2.2(i–j)
7. Intermolecular forces: induced and permanent dipole interactions — 2.2.2(k)
8. Hydrogen bonding — 2.2.2(l)
9. Hydrogen bonding and the properties of water and ice — 2.2.2(m)
10. Simple molecular lattices and their properties — 2.2.2(n–o)
