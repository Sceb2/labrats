## Quantities, units and symbols

### E001 · 3 marks

Match each electrical quantity to its meaning.

1. Which description defines current?
   - Energy transferred per unit charge
   - Energy transferred per second
   - Opposition to current
   - Rate of flow of electric charge

   **Marking point:** Current is the rate of flow of electric charge.

2. Which description defines potential difference?
   - Charge passing per second
   - Energy transferred per second
   - Total energy stored
   - Energy transferred per unit charge

   **Marking point:** Potential difference is energy transferred per unit charge.

3. Which description defines resistance?
   - Current multiplied by time
   - Power divided by time
   - Energy divided by charge
   - Potential difference divided by current

   **Marking point:** Resistance is the ratio of potential difference to current.

**Hint:** Distinguish an amount of energy from the rate of transfer.
